Three eras of astro research

1827-1960

The lone observer

Authorship held near 1.0 per paper for over a century. Solo work was simply how astronomy was done.

1960-1996

The acceleration

Co-authorship took off. By 1996, papers with five or more authors overtook single-author work for the first time.

1996-today

Big-team science

Solo papers fell to about 4%. The largest single paper now carries over 1,800 co-authors.
